  <h1 class="pageTitle">About Luke Dawnsey</h1>

  <p class="intro">System whisperer. Linux wrangler. DevOps artisan. I’m Luke—a craftsman who believes everything should run as smoothly as a Swiss watch… even if I had to build that watch from nuts, bash scripts, and a hearty dose of patience.</p>

  <p>This site is powered by <a href="http://jekyllrb.com">Jekyll</a>—because sometimes the most reliable things are the ones that have already stood the test of time. No flash, no frills—just substance, stability, and a touch of UNIX-style elegance.</p>

  <p>I’m a fan of simplicity in architecture, resilience in servers, and common sense in life. If it can be automated—I automate. If it can be made leaner—I’ll write a script that does it.</p>
