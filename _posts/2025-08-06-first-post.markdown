Hey there 👋

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I’m Luke “Dawnix” Dawnsey — a DevOps engineer, Linux enthusiast, and terminal tinkerer from Ukraine. This site is my digital workshop, portfolio, and lab notebook all rolled into one.

### 🛠 Why I built this site

This space is my attempt to **document the journey** and maybe, just maybe, help someone avoid a late-night `rm -rf /` disaster.

You'll find here:

- My favorite infrastructure-as-code projects
- Bash wizardry and terminal magic
- Lessons learned from Docker, Ansible, Terraform, and more
- Personal opinions on what makes a good dev setup (spoiler: `set -o errexit` is not optional)

### 🧪 Under the hood

This site runs on [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) using the [Long Haul theme](https://github.com/brianmaierjr/long-haul). They hosted on [Netlify](https://netlify.com).

Feel free to poke around, read, question, fork — or just say hi at [hello@dawnix.is-a.dev](mailto:hello@dawnix.is-a.dev).

Thanks for stopping by!
